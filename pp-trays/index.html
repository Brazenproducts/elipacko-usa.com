Product Overview

What Are PP Corrugated Plastic Trays?

PP corrugated plastic trays are shallow open-top containers fabricated from twin-wall polypropylene corrugated sheet. They are die-cut from flat sheet stock, scored, and folded — no molds, no tooling costs. Flat-pack knock-down design ships and stores efficiently; trays assemble in seconds on-site.

Available in standard retail and produce sizes or fully custom dimensions. Lightweight — typically 30–60% lighter than injection-molded plastic trays of equivalent size. Reusable 200–500 trips, 100% waterproof, pressure-washable. FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 food-safe in white and natural grades.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from US buyers sourcing pp corrugated plastic trays.

What is flat-pack knock-down (KD) tray design?
Flat-pack knock-down means the tray ships and stores as a flat die-cut blank. When needed, it is folded along pre-scored lines and the corner tabs lock in place — no tools, no glue. This reduces shipping volume by 80–90% vs pre-assembled trays, saving significant freight cost.
Are PP trays food-safe?
White and natural-colored PP corrugated trays comply with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for direct food contact. They are also USDA accepted for food facility use. Green, blue, and other colored trays using food-grade pigment are also food-safe — confirm pigment grade when ordering.
Can I get custom tray dimensions?
Yes — all dimensions are fully custom. Standard sheet sizes allow trays up to approximately 48 × 40 in. Elipacko's engineering team provides free CAD layout and physical prototype before production commitment.
How are PP trays cleaned?
Pressure-wash with water up to 160°F (71°C). Compatible with commercial detergents and mild sanitizers. Non-porous PP surface prevents bacterial growth. Do not use acetone or ketone solvents.
What is the lead time for custom printed trays?
3 days for standard sizes without printing. 14–21 days for custom sizes and/or custom printing (UV or screen print, up to 4 colors). Physical prototype ships before full production run.
