FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from US buyers sourcing pp corrugated plastic storage & moving boxes.

Are PP storage boxes better than cardboard for warehouse use?
Yes — for repeated-use warehouse applications, PP corrugated boxes outperform cardboard on every metric: 100% waterproof (cardboard fails when wet), 200–500 trip reuse life (cardboard is typically single-use), 50% lighter per unit, stackable under full load without crushing, and cleanable. Total cost of ownership is typically 80–90% lower than cardboard over the box lifetime.
Can PP moving boxes be rented?
Elipacko manufactures and sells PP moving boxes wholesale. For US rental fleets, contact Elipacko to discuss large-volume supply for rental operations — volume pricing available for 5,000+ unit orders.
Are PP storage boxes fire-safe for warehouse use?
PP corrugated sheet has a fire rating of UL 94 HB (horizontal burn). For warehouse environments with specific fire code requirements (NFPA 13, FM Global), consult your fire safety engineer. Flame-retardant PP corrugated grades are available on request.
What is the maximum stack height for PP storage boxes?
Stack height depends on box dimensions, wall thickness, and load weight. Elipacko engineering will provide stack rating calculations for your specific box and load. Standard 4mm grade boxes can typically stack 6–8 high at 30 lb per box — provide your requirements for a specific rating.
Can PP boxes be printed with barcodes and routing codes?
Yes. UV printing and screen printing are available for barcodes, QR codes, routing codes, department names, and company branding. Permanent label areas and barcode windows can also be designed into the box structure.
