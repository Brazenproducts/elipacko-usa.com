Applications

Industries That Use PP Corrugated Turnover Boxes

Trusted across food processing, agriculture, industrial, and commercial applications throughout the United States.

🚗

Automotive Manufacturing

PP turnover boxes are the standard returnable packaging in automotive JIT supply chains. Used for trim parts, fasteners, sensors, and body panels between Tier 1/2 suppliers and assembly plants. ESD-safe black for electronic components.

📱

Electronics Assembly

Antistatic black PP turnover boxes for PCB assemblies, display panels, and consumer electronics components. ESD-safe grade, non-particle-generating, compatible with cleanroom adjacent environments.

🥩

Food Processing

White FDA-safe PP turnover boxes for protein processing, bakery, and prepared food applications. Pressure-washable, non-porous, USDA accepted. Replaces single-use wax cardboard in cold chain environments.

🏭

Industrial Distribution

Heavy-duty PP turnover boxes for industrial fasteners, tools, and MRO supplies. Color-coded by department or supplier for visual management. Forklift-base models for high-volume operations.

🌾

Agriculture & Produce

Ventilated PP turnover crates for produce harvesting, packing, and retail distribution. Stack 8–12 high on pallets. USDA accepted, FDA food-safe, pressure-washable.

💊

Pharmaceutical & Medical

Clean PP turnover boxes for pharmaceutical supply chains. Non-porous, chemical-resistant, no off-gassing. Easy to sanitize between loads. Audit trail compatible (barcode/RFID label areas).
