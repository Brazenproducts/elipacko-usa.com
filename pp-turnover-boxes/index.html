FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from US buyers sourcing pp corrugated turnover boxes.

What is the difference between a turnover box and a standard shipping box?
A turnover box (also called a returnable tote or circulation box) is designed for multi-trip closed-loop use — it makes 300–500 round trips between supplier and customer. A standard shipping box is typically one-way/single-use. Turnover boxes have heavier wall thickness (5–10mm vs 3–4mm), reinforced corners, and are designed for long-term reuse rather than disposal after delivery.
Can PP turnover boxes be used in automotive JIT supply chains?
Yes — PP corrugated turnover boxes are widely used in automotive JIT (just-in-time) and kanban supply chains. They are compatible with standard automotive container standards (VDA, KLT footprints available on request). ESD-safe black grade is available for electronic assemblies.
Are PP turnover boxes compatible with standard pallet racking?
Yes. Turnover boxes are available with GMA 48×40 in base footprint and forklift-entry bases for racking compatibility. Custom footprints are also available. Load ratings are clearly marked on each box per OSHA 1910.176.
Can I get turnover boxes with integrated PP divider inserts?
Yes. Elipacko manufactures turnover box and divider insert systems as a matched set — the divider cells are configured to your part dimensions and slot into the box. Quote the box dimensions, part dimensions, and number of cells and Elipacko engineering handles the rest.
What is the ROI of switching from single-use cardboard to PP turnover boxes?
For most applications, PP turnover boxes achieve payback within 8–15 round trips vs single-use cardboard. At 300–500 trip cycle life, total packaging cost reduction is typically 80–90%. Additional savings from reduced disposal cost, reduced damage claims, and lower freight volume on return loads.
