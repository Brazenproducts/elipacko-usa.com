Overview

What Are PP Corrugated Boxes?

PP corrugated boxes are rigid shipping and storage containers fabricated from twin-wall polypropylene corrugated sheet — the same base material as corrugated cardboard, but made from polypropylene plastic instead of paper fiber. The twin-wall flute structure (two flat faces bonded to an internal fluted layer) delivers exceptional rigidity relative to weight, making PP corrugated the strongest waterproof packaging material at comparable weight to cardboard.

The defining advantage over cardboard is moisture performance. A standard cardboard box loses 40–70% of its compression strength when wet. A PP corrugated box retains 100% of rated stack strength regardless of moisture exposure — wet, dry, submerged, or condensation-laden cold chain environments make no difference. That structural reliability is why PP corrugated has become the standard in fresh produce, seafood, meat, and pharmaceutical cold chain logistics worldwide.

How Twin-Wall PP Corrugated Is Made

PP corrugated sheet is manufactured by co-extruding polypropylene into a continuous twin-wall flute profile — two solid PP face sheets bonded to a corrugated inner flute layer. This is done in a single extrusion pass, producing a void-core structure that is 70–80% air by volume but structurally rigid in compression. Sheet thickness runs from 2mm (lightweight inserts) to 12mm (heavy structural panels). Boxes are cut and scored from flat sheet — no molds, no tooling, no minimum run size constraints from tooling investment.

PP Corrugated Box FAQs

What are PP corrugated boxes used for?

Fresh produce, seafood, meat, poultry, pharmaceutical, automotive parts, and industrial shipping. They replace one-way cardboard in closed-loop supply chains where boxes are returned and reused 200–500 times.

How do PP corrugated boxes compare to cardboard?

PP corrugated is 100% waterproof and reusable 200–500 trips. Cardboard loses 40–70% compression strength when wet. At 300 reuse trips, PP costs 90–97% less per trip than cardboard.

Are PP corrugated boxes FDA food-safe?

Yes — virgin PP corrugated complies with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for direct food contact. USDA accepted for fresh produce, meat, poultry, and seafood.

What is the anti-dumping duty on PP boxes from Thailand?

0%. Thailand-manufactured PP corrugated boxes enter the US with no anti-dumping duty. Note: China-manufactured PP corrugated boxes are subject to AD/CVD duties as of January 2026.

What wall thickness for produce boxes?

4mm for lightweight produce under 30 lb. 6mm for standard 44–66 lb loads. 8mm for heavy root vegetables, melons, and industrial applications above 66 lb.

What is the minimum order quantity?

500 pieces for standard sizes. Custom sizes and printing start at 500 units, 3-day production. One 40HQ container is typical for a full custom specification.
