Frequently Asked Questions

How many times can the Velcro closure be opened and closed?

Industrial-grade Velcro (hook and loop) is rated for 5,000+ open/close cycles under normal conditions. With proper care and cleaning, closures on PP corrugated boxes can last significantly longer.

Can the box be made dustproof or splash resistant?

The PP corrugated body is fully waterproof. The Velcro closure provides a reasonable seal against dust and light moisture. For full IP-rated sealing, we recommend adding a foam gasket strip around the lid perimeter — available as a custom option.

Can I get custom printing on the box?

Yes — UV printing and silk-screen printing are available on all PP corrugated boxes including velcro closure versions. Company logos, part numbers, warning labels and handling instructions can all be printed directly on the box surface.

What Is a Velcro Closure PP Corrugated Box?

A Velcro Closure PP Corrugated Plastic Box is a reusable packaging and storage container made from twin-wall polypropylene corrugated sheet, featuring an integrated hook-and-loop (Velcro) closure system on the lid. The Velcro closure allows the box to be opened and closed quickly and repeatedly — thousands of times — without tape, clips, clasps or complicated locking hardware. This makes it ideal for any application requiring frequent access to contents, including industrial parts storage, production line material handling, warehouse management, document organization and electronics packaging.

The core material — PP corrugated sheet — provides a waterproof, lightweight, and structurally rigid container body that can be custom manufactured in any size, thickness or color. The Velcro closure can be positioned on one or multiple sides, and can be upgraded with foam gaskets for dust exclusion or with reinforcement for applications requiring more secure closure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of Velcro are available for the closure?

We offer three Velcro integration methods: (1) Self-adhesive Velcro strips bonded to the PP surface — suitable for lighter applications and lower cycle counts. (2) Heat-sealed Velcro — bonded more permanently using heat fusion for high-cycle industrial applications. (3) Sewn-through Velcro with reinforcement tape — the most durable option, rated for 10,000+ cycles, used in demanding logistics applications.

Can the box be made tamper-evident?

The Velcro closure itself is not tamper-evident. For applications requiring tamper evidence, we recommend adding a secondary clip or zip-tie attachment point alongside the Velcro closure, or using a separate security seal slot in the box lid. Full tamper-evident solutions are available on request.

What is the maximum weight the box can hold?

Load capacity depends on the box dimensions and PP board thickness. A standard 400×300×150mm box in 4mm PP can hold 10-15kg. For heavy loads, we recommend 5mm or 6mm board with aluminum corner reinforcement. Provide us with your required dimensions and load and we will specify the appropriate construction.

Can interior foam inserts or dividers be added?

Yes — custom foam inserts (PE, EVA or polyurethane foam) profiled to hold specific products can be added inside the Velcro closure box. PP corrugated divider partitions can also be added to create multiple compartments. Combination of foam base + PP dividers is common for electronics and instrument packaging.

How do I clean and maintain the Velcro closure?

The PP box body can be wiped down or pressure-washed. For Velcro maintenance, use a soft brush or lint roller to remove debris from the hook side — accumulated debris reduces closure strength over time. Do not use solvents on Velcro as this degrades the adhesive bonding. Replace Velcro strips if closure strength falls below acceptable level — strips are available as a spare part.
