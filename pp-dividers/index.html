PP vs Alternatives

Why PP Corrugated Dividers Beat Cardboard and Foam

PP corrugated outperforms cardboard dividers and foam inserts on every metric that matters for US buyers.

PP vs Cardboard Dividers

Cardboard dividers absorb moisture, collapse under load when wet, and are single-use. PP corrugated dividers are 100% waterproof, retain structural rigidity in cold chain environments, and return 200–500 times — reducing per-trip cost by up to 90% vs cardboard.

PP vs Foam Inserts

Foam inserts crumble, off-gas VOCs in food applications, and cannot be sanitized. PP corrugated dividers are non-porous, pressure-washable to 160°F, FDA food-safe, and generate zero particle contamination. Reusable vs single-use.

PP vs Thermoformed Plastic

Thermoformed trays require expensive tooling and long lead times. PP corrugated dividers are cut and scored from flat sheet — zero tooling cost, 3-day production, custom cell size changes with no additional investment.

FAQ

PP Corrugated Divider FAQs

Common questions from US buyers sourcing PP corrugated plastic dividers.

What thickness PP corrugated sheet is best for dividers?
4mm is the most common for medium-duty applications (automotive trim, electronics, produce). 3mm works for lightweight parts; 5mm or 6mm for heavy industrial components or high-stack trays. Elipacko's engineering team will recommend the right thickness based on your part weight and reuse cycle requirements.
Can PP dividers be custom printed?
Yes. Elipacko offers UV printing and screen printing on PP corrugated dividers — part numbers, barcodes, handling instructions, or brand colors. Custom print is available from MOQ 500 sets with 14–21 day lead time for the first run.
What is the minimum order quantity for custom cell sizes?
500 sets for custom cell configurations with existing sheet stock. New die/cut patterns may require 1,000 unit MOQ for the first run. Standard cell grid configurations (6×3, 5×4, 4×4, etc.) are available at 500 unit MOQ with 3-day production.
Are PP dividers safe for direct food contact?
White and natural-colored PP corrugated dividers comply with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 for direct food contact. They are also USDA accepted for food facility use. Black ESD-grade PP is NOT recommended for direct food contact — black carbon-black pigment is not FDA food-safe.
How do I clean and sanitize PP corrugated dividers?
PP corrugated dividers can be pressure-washed with water up to 160°F (71°C). Compatible with most commercial detergents, sanitizers, and mild bleach solutions. Non-porous PP surface prevents bacterial growth and odor absorption. Do not use solvents (acetone, MEK) which can degrade PP over time.
