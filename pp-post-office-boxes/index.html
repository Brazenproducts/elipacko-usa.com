FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from US buyers sourcing PP corrugated post office boxes and postal totes.

How do PP postal totes compare to cardboard mail trays?
PP corrugated postal totes are waterproof, crush-resistant, and reusable 200–500 times vs. single-use cardboard. Over a 3-year period, PP totes cost 60–80% less per delivery cycle than replacing cardboard continuously. They also maintain their shape when wet — a critical advantage in outdoor last-mile delivery environments.
Are these the same totes used by national postal services?
Yes. PP corrugated postal totes are the standard reusable tote format used by postal and courier services globally, replacing wood, wire, and single-use cardboard. Elipacko produces to postal service dimensional standards and can match existing tote specs exactly.
Can totes be color-coded for route or department sorting?
Yes. PP corrugated totes are available in any color — yellow, red, blue, green, orange, white, or custom. Color-coding is a standard practice in postal sorting operations. Custom print (route codes, facility logos, barcodes) is also available.
What is the nestable design and why does it matter?
Nestable PP totes have a slightly tapered body — when empty, they stack inside one another, reducing storage and return-trip volume by 30–40%. For delivery depots managing return logistics, this translates directly to fewer return trips and lower fuel costs.
What is the minimum order quantity?
500 units for standard sizes. For large postal network deployments requiring tens of thousands of units, contact Elipacko for volume pricing and lead time — production capacity is 50,000+ tons annually.
