Applications

Industries That Use PP Corrugated Post Office Boxes

Used wherever mail, parcels, and documents need to be sorted, transported, and distributed efficiently.

📬

Postal Sorting Facilities

High-visibility yellow PP totes for mail sorting lines — color-coded by route, zip code, or mail class. Lightweight enough for repeated manual handling across full shifts.

🚚

Last-Mile Delivery Depots

Nestable PP postal totes for courier and parcel depot operations. Nest compactly when empty for return logistics — saves significant truck space on return runs.

🏢

Corporate Mail Rooms

Reusable PP totes for internal inter-departmental mail routing. Color-code by department or floor. Replace disposable cardboard boxes permanently.

📦

E-Commerce Fulfillment

Lightweight PP totes for pick-and-pack and outbound sortation in fulfillment centers. Smooth interior walls protect items; open-top design speeds loading.

🏥

Healthcare & Labs

Waterproof PP totes for specimen transport, pharmacy distribution, and inter-facility document routing. Wipe-clean surfaces; chemical-resistant PP material.

🔄

Returnable Tote Programs

Reusable PP postal totes as a direct replacement for single-use cardboard in closed-loop distribution networks. 200–500 trip lifespan vs. single-use cardboard.
