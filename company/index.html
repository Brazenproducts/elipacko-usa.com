Who We Are

Elipacko is one of Asia's largest manufacturers of PP corrugated plastic packaging. Our flagship facility is located in Thailand, giving US importers a direct line to zero anti-dumping duty on all products.

We manufacture everything in-house — from raw polypropylene resin to finished boxes, gaylords, pallets, meat lugs, poultry crates, and corrugated sheets. No middlemen. No markups. Factory direct.

Why Thailand Manufacturing?

Thai-manufactured PP corrugated products enter the US at 0% anti-dumping duty, compared to significant ADD surcharges on Chinese-origin goods. Elipacko's Thailand facility gives US buyers a fully compliant, cost-effective supply chain.

USA manufacturing capacity is also in active development — domestic production will eliminate import duties entirely for qualifying orders.
