FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from US buyers sourcing pp corrugated plastic ballot boxes.

Are PP corrugated ballot boxes tamper-evident?
PP corrugated ballot boxes include designed tamper-evident features: security slot sized to paper ballots (preventing hand insertion), lid flaps with seal strip areas for official tamper-evident stickers, and serial number label areas. Additional security options including numbered stickers and lockable hasp brackets are available on request.
How do PP ballot boxes compare to wood or steel?
PP corrugated ballot boxes are 30–50% lighter than wood equivalents and 70–80% lighter than steel. They flat-pack to 1/5 the volume for pre-election storage and transport. They are waterproof, rot-proof, and reusable across multiple election cycles — total cost of ownership is significantly lower than wood over 3+ election cycles.
Can boxes be custom printed with official commission seals?
Yes. Elipacko offers UV printing and screen printing in up to full color. Election commissions provide artwork (logo, seal, station code format) and Elipacko produces print-ready files for approval before production. Physical color proofs available on request.
What is the minimum order quantity?
500 units for standard ballot box sizes with or without custom print. For national elections requiring tens of thousands of units, contact Elipacko for volume pricing — production capacity is 50,000+ tons annually.
Are PP ballot boxes reusable across multiple elections?
Yes. PP corrugated ballot boxes are reusable 200–500 trips. With proper storage (flat-packed, dry environment), boxes can be reused across multiple election cycles, significantly reducing per-election cost compared to single-use alternatives.
