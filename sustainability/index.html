Sustainability at Elipacko

Reusable, recyclable polypropylene packaging designed to replace single-use cardboard — lowering waste, cost, and environmental footprint across the supply chain.

Polypropylene packaging isn't just more durable than cardboard — it's built to be used again and again. Reuse is the core of our sustainability story.

100% Recyclable Polypropylene

Our packaging is made from virgin PP — fully recyclable at end of life, with no blended or laminated materials that contaminate recycling streams.

Built for Reuse

PP corrugated boxes, gaylords, and pallets are washable and reusable for hundreds of trips, replacing single-use cardboard and cutting waste per shipment.

Washable & Water-Resistant

Unlike cardboard, our boxes don't absorb water or degrade — they're cleaned and returned to service instead of discarded, reducing replacement volume.

Lower Lifetime Footprint

Reusable packaging spreads its environmental cost across many trips, lowering the per-use carbon and material footprint versus disposable alternatives.

Efficient Shipping

Collapsible and nestable designs reduce return-transport volume, meaning fewer trucks and lower emissions on the reverse logistics leg.

Responsible Manufacturing

In-house production from resin to finished goods means tighter quality control, less scrap, and a shorter, more accountable supply chain.
