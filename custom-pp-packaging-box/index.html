What Is a Custom PP Corrugated Packaging Box?

A custom PP corrugated packaging box is a reusable container manufactured from twin-wall polypropylene corrugated sheet and designed specifically to the requirements of a particular brand or product. Every dimension, color, closure style, internal configuration and printed element is specified by the customer and manufactured to match their exact requirements.

Custom PP corrugated packaging is chosen by brands that need packaging which does more than simply hold a product — it must protect reliably across multiple transit cycles, represent the company's brand standards, work efficiently in warehouse and distribution environments, and justify its cost through reuse. At 300 use cycles, a custom PP box costs 99% less per use than a comparable single-use cardboard box.

Customization Options

Dimensions & Structure

Any internal or external dimension. Box structure options: regular slotted container (RSC), full overlap (FOL), die-cut self-erecting, tray with separate lid, drawer-style and folding flat-pack designs. Internal dimensions are designed around your product with tolerance margins for safe product movement during transit.

Color & Printing

PP corrugated available in any base color matched to Pantone or RAL specifications. Printing options: UV flatbed (photographic quality, multi-color), silk-screen (solid colors and logos, durable for high-cycle reuse), and hot stamping (metallic finishes for premium packaging). Print coverage can be one or both sides.

Closure Systems

Closure options: folded flap (no hardware), Velcro hook-and-loop, plastic click-clips, magnetic closure, hinged lid with lock, and cord closure. The right closure depends on access frequency, security requirements and the product contents. We recommend Velcro for frequent-access reusable packaging and clip closures for secure transport.

Interior Configuration

Interior options include plain open box, PP corrugated divider partitions, foam inserts profiled to product shape (PE foam, EVA foam, polyurethane foam), and combined foam base with PP divider cells. For multi-product sets, we design compartmented interiors that hold each item securely. Labels, part numbers and assembly guides can be printed on internal surfaces.
