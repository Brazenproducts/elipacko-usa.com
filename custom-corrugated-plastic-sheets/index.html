What Are Custom Corrugated Plastic Sheets?

Custom corrugated plastic sheets are twin-wall polypropylene corrugated panels manufactured to customer-specified dimensions, shapes, thicknesses and colors. Unlike standard full-size sheets (1220x2440mm), custom sheets are precision-cut using CNC routers, die cutters or guillotine cutters to produce specific-size components for packaging, signage, construction protection, agricultural and industrial applications.

PP corrugated can be cut into rectangles, squares, circles, L-shapes, T-shapes or virtually any profile — including unusual shapes like the guitar backboards shown in our product photos. It can be scored, folded, slotted, perforated, printed and laminated to create finished components or sub-assemblies for a wide range of applications across virtually every industry.

Custom Sheet Applications

Packaging Inserts & Dividers

Custom-cut sheets and die-cut partition inserts for corrugated boxes, shipping cases and molded pulp packaging. PP corrugated inserts replace cardboard dividers with a reusable, waterproof alternative that maintains its shape and protective function across hundreds of use cycles. Common for bottle packing, glass protection, electronics packaging and automotive component separation.

Signage & Display Boards

PP corrugated is the global standard substrate for temporary signage, real estate signs, election signs and retail POS displays. Custom-cut panels in any shape can be UV printed with full-color graphics. UV-stabilized grades resist outdoor weathering. Available pre-colored in white, black, yellow, red, blue and custom Pantone colors.

Construction Protection

Temporary floor protection, countertop protection, wall protection and hoarding boards during construction and renovation. Custom-cut panels cover specific surfaces exactly, and the waterproof, cleanable PP corrugated surface protects finishes from paint, concrete splatter and foot traffic. Common thicknesses: 4mm (light) and 6mm (heavy traffic).

Agricultural Applications

PP corrugated sheets are widely used in greenhouse and outdoor agricultural applications: seedling tray bases, greenhouse sidewall panels, row cover support, reflective mulch underlays and pest barrier strips. UV-stabilized grade withstands outdoor weathering and resists chemical sprays and fertilizers.
