Material Properties

Why Polypropylene Corrugated

vs. Cardboard Corrugated

  • 100% waterproof — no moisture absorption
  • Reusable 10–50× vs single-use cardboard
  • Chemical resistant — unaffected by most solvents
  • Consistent compression strength when wet
  • 100% recyclable, closed-loop PP recovery

vs. Solid Plastic Sheet

PP corrugated sheet weighs 70–80% less than solid PP sheet of equivalent thickness. The fluted structure provides disproportionate rigidity relative to weight — ideal for packaging and signage where every gram of freight cost matters.

Technical Properties — PP Corrugated Sheet

Polypropylene corrugated sheet (also sold under trade names Coroplast, Correx, Twinplast, and Corriflute) is a twin-wall extruded polypropylene board with internal flutes running parallel to the sheet length. The fluted core delivers exceptional rigidity-to-weight ratio compared to any other plastic sheet product at equivalent thickness.

Thickness Selection Guide

Choosing the wrong thickness is the most common error in corrugated plastic sheet sourcing. Undersized sheets flex and deform under load; oversized sheets add unnecessary freight cost. Use this guide as a starting point — for structural or load-bearing applications, always prototype before committing to full production quantity.

2mm — Lightweight & Print

Mailer inserts, catalog dividers, election leaflet backing, lightweight point-of-purchase trays. Not suitable for any structural or load-bearing use.

3mm — Standard Signage

Yard signs, real estate signs, political campaign signs, indoor POS displays. The industry-standard substrate for 4-color print-and-display applications.

4mm — Reusable Packaging

Part dividers, returnable trays, automotive component packaging. Handles 20–50 trip cycles. The most commonly specified corrugated plastic sheet gauge globally.

5mm — Agriculture & Structural

Seedling flat bases, greenhouse wall panels, cold room shelf liners, corrugated plastic box blanks. Adequate compression strength for single-stack pallet configurations.

6mm — Industrial Dividers

Heavy-duty packaging inserts, industrial cell dividers, pallet layer pads for components over 10kg per cell. Standard gauge for automotive OEM returnable packaging.

8–12mm — Heavy Industrial

Pallet bases, floor protection panels, cold-chain box side walls, structural load boards. 12mm is the maximum standard gauge; custom laminated configurations available beyond 12mm.

Frequently Asked Questions — Corrugated Plastic Sheets

What is the difference between PP corrugated sheet and Coroplast?

Coroplast is a brand name for corrugated polypropylene sheet, the same material as "PP corrugated sheet," "Correx," "Twinplast," and "Corriflute." All are twin-wall extruded PP board with internal flutes. The material is identical; the trade names differ by manufacturer and region. Elipacko manufactures to the same specifications as branded alternatives at significantly lower cost due to Thailand manufacturing and 0% anti-dumping duty.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Standard colors (black, white, grey) at standard sizes: 100 sheets minimum. Custom colors, custom sizes, and specialty grades (FR, anti-static, food-grade): 500 sheets minimum per SKU. Full container (FCL) orders receive best pricing — approximately 8,000–12,000 standard sheets per 20' container depending on thickness. Sample orders of 5–10 sheets are available for qualification before committing to production.

Are Elipacko corrugated plastic sheets food-safe?

Yes. Elipacko's food-contact grade PP corrugated sheets comply with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 (indirect food contact) and EU Regulation 10/2011 (plastic materials in contact with food). These grades use virgin PP resin with food-approved colorants and UV stabilizers. They are widely used in produce packaging, seafood boxes, poultry trays, and cold-chain containers. Non-food-contact grades use recycled PP content and are not appropriate for direct food contact applications.

How long do PP corrugated sheets last outdoors?

Standard UV-stabilized grade: 12–18 months outdoor exposure at full sun in temperate climates before significant brittling. Premium UV grade: 3–5 years. PP degrades under UV exposure — for permanent outdoor structural applications, HDPE or polycarbonate is more appropriate. For temporary outdoor signage (real estate, events, political campaigns), 3mm standard UV grade is adequate for a 6–12 month campaign life.

Can corrugated plastic sheets be thermoformed or heat-bent?

Yes. PP corrugated sheet can be scored and folded cold for simple box blanks and trays. For curved or complex shapes, the sheet can be heat-formed at 150–160°C — the flute structure partially collapses during forming, which reduces panel rigidity in the bent zone. For structural curved parts, solid PP sheet or HDPE is more appropriate. Elipacko can produce flat sheet blanks scored and perforated ready for fold-and-glue box assembly.

What is the lead time for corrugated plastic sheet orders?

Standard color and size: 15–21 days production + transit. Custom color or grade: 21–35 days production + transit. Sea freight from Thailand to US West Coast: 18–22 days. Air freight available for urgent orders — typically 5–7 days delivery. Full container orders are eligible for faster production scheduling. Contact our team for exact lead times on your specific quantity and specification.
